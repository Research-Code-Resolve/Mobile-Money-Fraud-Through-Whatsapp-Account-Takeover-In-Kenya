## 🎤 ANTICIPATED QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

**Q: How do you prevent false/malicious reports?**
A: "Great question. We have multiple safeguards: reports require evidence, we have a community voting system, reports are weighted by user reputation, and flagged reports go through admin verification before being shown prominently."

**Q: What about privacy? People might not want to share fraud experiences.**
A: "Privacy is core to our design. All reporting can be done anonymously, we comply with Kenya's Data Protection Act, and users control what information they share. The goal is to protect the community while respecting individual privacy."

**Q: How will you monetize this?**
A: "Phase 1 is completely free to build the user base and community data. Phase 2 introduces a premium tier with advanced AI features for 99 KES/month, and Phase 3 targets businesses with an API for bulk fraud checking. The core fraud prevention will always remain free."

**Q: Where's the backend? How does data persist?**
A: "The frontend is complete and currently uses local storage for demo purposes. We have comprehensive API documentation ready for backend integration, which we estimate will take 1-2 weeks once the backend team is assigned. All endpoints are specified in our BACKEND_API_REQUIREMENTS.md file."

**Q: How does the AI fraud detection work?**
A: "Currently, we use pattern matching against known fraud indicators - urgency language, money requests, unfamiliar numbers. The roadmap includes machine learning models trained on real fraud reports from our community database. As we collect more data, the AI becomes smarter."

**Q: Why a mobile app instead of a website?**
A: "Mobile-first is critical because 89% of Kenyans access the internet primarily through smartphones, and fraud happens on mobile. WhatsApp is mobile, M-Pesa is mobile - our solution needed to be where the fraud happens."

**Q: What makes this different from existing solutions?**
A: "Three things: ONE - we're prevention, not reaction. You check BEFORE sending money. TWO - community intelligence. One report protects thousands. THREE - the safe word system. No one else has this family verification layer."
